About us

Get to know Awtad Alnokhbah Trading Co. Ltd.

Our story

Company profile

Awtad Alnokhbah Trading Co. Ltd was founded in Sudan in 2026 to provide integrated solutions in livestock and agricultural production, and aims to be the trusted partner of farmers and breeders across Sudan.

Our range covers all types of feed, feed manufacturing inputs, veterinary medicines and agrochemicals. We import agricultural and industrial inputs and export our products to many countries.

The name “Awtad” (pegs) stands for stability and growth, which is what we aim to build with our farmer and breeder partners: a sustainable, thriving agricultural sector.

Our vision

To be the leading livestock and agricultural production company in Sudan and the region, contributing to food security and sustainable development.

Our mission

To deliver high-quality products and services that meet the needs of farmers and breeders, committed to quality and sustainability, and supporting agricultural development in Sudan.

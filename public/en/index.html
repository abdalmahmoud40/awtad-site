About us

Awtad Alnokhbah Trading Co. Ltd

A Sudanese company specialising in livestock and agricultural production, founded in 2026 in Wad Madani to provide farmers and breeders across Sudan with the best products and services.

We supply feeds and their manufacturing inputs, veterinary medicines and agrochemicals; we import agricultural and industrial inputs and export our products to many countries.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What products does Awtad Alnokhbah supply?

Poultry and cattle feeds; feed manufacturing inputs such as lysine, protein concentrates and premixes; veterinary medicines; and agricultural pesticides and fertilizers.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote request form or message us on WhatsApp at +249 91 234 0786 with the product, quantity and delivery location, and we will reply with an offer.

Do you deliver outside Wad Madani?

Yes. Our distribution network covers Gezira, Khartoum, White Nile, Blue Nile, Sennar, Gedaref, Kassala and Red Sea states.

Do you sell wholesale to distributors and feed mills?

Yes. We supply feed mills, farms and distributors in bulk, and welcome distributor applications through the Partnerships page.

Are you active in import and export?

Yes. We import agricultural and industrial inputs and export our products to many countries. Contact us to discuss your needs.

Do you provide technical support to breeders and farmers?

Yes. Our team gives technical guidance on choosing the right product and how to use it.
